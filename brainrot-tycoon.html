Brainrot Tycoon — Android Privacy Policy

Updated: 20 October 2026. Publisher: Digital Rat AI / Brain Rat Games. Android package: com.babarobat.brainrottycoon. Contact: digital.rat.ai@gmail.com. Website: Brain Rat Games.

Scope and versions

This policy covers the Android edition of Brainrot Tycoon distributed through Google Play. Version 1.0.2 adds Google AdMob advertising, Google UMP advertising privacy choices, optional Firebase gameplay analytics and a button that opens the app's Google Play page. The earlier Android version 1.0.1 contains no advertising or analytics SDK. Browser editions hosted by other platforms use their own platform services and privacy policies.

Local progress and your choices

You can play without creating an account. Game progress, discoveries, audio/language settings and privacy choices are stored in Android app preferences on your device. We do not upload game saves or operate cloud saves. Android backup and device-transfer backup of app data are disabled. Artwork, fonts, music and sound effects are bundled, so ordinary gameplay works offline. This edition has no in-app purchases, online leaderboard, chat, camera or microphone feature.

At first launch you can choose an age band or continue without collection. The age-band answer stays on the device; it is not sent as a gameplay analytics dimension. Advertising and gameplay analytics are disabled for players choosing 13–15 or not providing an age answer. Players choosing 16+ can separately opt into gameplay analytics. The analytics checkbox is off by default. You can revisit these choices through Settings → Age & analytics choices, including choosing Continue without collection. Declining does not prevent ordinary play.

Advertising and advertising privacy

For eligible players, Google AdMob supplies optional rewarded advertisements and interstitial advertisements at selected transitions. Ad requests start only after the game's age check and Google UMP's advertising permission check. Advertising privacy choices are separate from the optional gameplay analytics checkbox. Use Settings → Ad privacy options to review the advertising choices available for your region, including consent withdrawal or sale/sharing opt-out where applicable.

The app requests nonpersonalized ads and applies Google's teen ad treatment and a PG maximum ad-content rating to all eligible ad requests. This does not mean advertising involves no data processing. Google Mobile Ads can collect and share IP-derived approximate location, app/ad interactions such as launches, taps and video views, technical diagnostics and device/app identifiers for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention and security. The developer does not request precise location. Google's SDK and privacy choices control the identifiers used for a given request. No third-party mediation SDKs are added by this edition.

Google UMP can process request information to determine the relevant privacy message and remember advertising choices. Google and its advertising partners process advertising information under their applicable policies, including Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information for advertising. Choosing nonpersonalized ads or declining gameplay analytics does not by itself disable all advertising measurement.

Optional gameplay analytics

Only after an eligible player's explicit opt-in, Google Analytics for Firebase receives a limited set of gameplay and performance events. These include sessions, plant/unit purchases with in-game currency, merges, upgrades, discoveries, rewards, progress, selected screens and basic frame-performance measurements. Parameters describe game content, amounts, language, device class and app version; we do not attach a name, email address, account name, date of birth or developer-managed user ID.

Firebase can assign an app-instance identifier, process lifecycle/session information and derive approximate geography from masked IP addresses. Analytics advertising-ID collection, SSAID collection and automatic screen reporting are disabled. Analytics ad storage, ad-user-data and ad-personalization consent remain denied even when gameplay analytics is allowed. No Crashlytics SDK is included. Custom gameplay events before opt-in are discarded rather than saved for later transmission.

The Analytics account has Google products and services data sharing enabled. Google may therefore also use shared Analytics information for its own products and services under the applicable Google terms and privacy controls; we do not describe every transfer as solely processing on the publisher's behalf. We use the reports to understand gameplay and improve the game.

Withdrawing gameplay analytics permission stops future game telemetry and disables collection through the integration. It also resets the local Analytics identifier. This does not automatically erase information already received by Google or previously included in reports. The Analytics properties are configured for two months of event-data retention and fourteen months of user-data retention, with the user retention period reset by new activity. These settings do not describe the retention of every aggregated report or Google's independently processed service data. Google Signals and Analytics advertising personalization are disabled. Contact us for privacy or deletion requests; we may need enough information to identify the relevant records, and some aggregated or provider-held information may not be individually retrievable.

Other network activity and security

Google SDKs encrypt transmitted user data using TLS. Google Play separately processes installation, distribution, security and diagnostic information under Google's policies and your Google/device settings. The rating button opens Google Play, which controls any account or review information you choose to submit. Opening policy/support links or an advertiser destination can send ordinary web request information to the relevant service. The app does not read your Google password or publish a review automatically.

The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, precise location or shared-photo access. Its Internet/network permissions support advertising, privacy messages, optional analytics and external links. Advertising identifiers and other SDK identifiers are distinct from your local game save.

Deleting local data and support

Clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall it to remove local progress, settings and saved privacy choices. This resets the game on that device; it does not delete information already processed by Google. Google provides advertising-ID and account/privacy controls in Android and its services.

If you email digital.rat.ai@gmail.com, we use your address and message to answer your request. Do not send passwords or unnecessary personal documents. You may request deletion of support correspondence through the same address; information may be retained where needed to resolve a dispute or meet applicable obligations.

Age and changes

The Android game is intended for players aged 13 and older. The on-device choice described above disables advertising and analytics for under-16 or unknown-age choices. We update this policy and Google Play declarations when the app's data practices change. The date above identifies this policy version.
