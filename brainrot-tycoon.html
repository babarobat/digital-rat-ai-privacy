Brainrot Tycoon — Android Privacy Policy

Updated: 20 October 2026. Publisher: Digital Rat AI. Android package: com.babarobat.brainrottycoon. Contact: digital.rat.ai@gmail.com.

Scope

This policy describes the offline Android edition of Brainrot Tycoon distributed through Google Play. Browser editions hosted by other platforms may use that platform's services and privacy policy.

Local gameplay and settings

You can play without creating an account. Game progress, discoveries and audio/language settings are stored on your device using Android app preferences. The game does not upload these saves to the developer or operate cloud saves. Android backup and device-transfer backup of app data are disabled in this edition.

Data collection and sharing

This Android edition contains no advertising SDK, gameplay analytics, online leaderboard, in-app purchase SDK or account sign-in. The game does not collect or transmit personal data to the developer, request an advertising identifier, or sell or share personal information. Game artwork, fonts, music and sound effects are bundled with the app. Ordinary gameplay does not require a network connection.

Google Play may separately process installation, purchase-free distribution, security and diagnostic information under Google's Privacy Policy and your Google account/device settings. Opening the privacy-policy link launches a website in your browser; the website hosting provider can process normal web request information under its own policy. This is separate from gameplay and local saves.

Permissions

The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, precise location or shared-file access. Its Android Internet permission supports the app's standard WebView runtime; it does not enable advertising or developer analytics.

Deleting local data

To remove game progress and settings, clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall it. The developer has no server-side copy to retrieve or delete. Clearing app storage resets progress on that device.

Support

If you email digital.rat.ai@gmail.com, we use your email address and message to answer your request. Do not send passwords or unnecessary personal documents. You can ask us to delete support correspondence by contacting the same address; we may retain information where needed to resolve a dispute or meet applicable obligations.

Changes

If the Android edition's data practices change, this policy and the app's Google Play declarations will be updated. The date above identifies this policy version.
