Metal Mice — Privacy Policy

Updated: 16 October 2026. Publisher: Digital Rat AI. Android package and iOS bundle ID: com.babarobat.metalmice. Contact: digital.rat.ai@gmail.com.

Gameplay and local saves

Metal Mice can be played without creating a game account. Progress, career statistics, and audio/language settings are stored on your device. The Android and iOS games do not use cloud saves. Browser saves are stored in your browser; clearing site data removes them. Hosted-platform saves may use the hosting platform storage. Optional developer gameplay analytics is described below. It does not include in-app purchases. Clear the app's storage in Android settings or delete the app to remove local saves. On iOS, deleting the app removes its local progress; offloading the app may retain it.

Optional gameplay analytics and feedback

Versions of Metal Mice with developer analytics offer an explicit, optional choice before sending gameplay analytics. You can decline and continue playing, or change the choice in Settings. On the browser edition, Google Analytics is loaded only after you allow analytics. On Android and iOS, Firebase Analytics is initialized and enabled only after the saved analytics choice allows it. Events from before consent are not replayed when you later opt in. Turning analytics off stops new developer analytics events and clears pending unsent gameplay events.

When enabled, Google Analytics / Firebase receives events such as session starts and duration, screens and settings, selected characters and tracks, race results and progress, upgrade purchases using in-game coins, rewards, leaderboard or rating-flow outcomes, and optional feedback from fixed sentiment and topic choices. Event details include game version, platform, language, device category, session or run identifiers, and gameplay counters. We use this information to understand game balance, progression and which features players like or find frustrating. The gameplay event payload does not include player names, email addresses, Google Play Games account IDs, support messages, or free-text feedback.

Google Analytics uses a pseudonymous browser/client or app-instance identifier, device/app information and IP-derived approximate location. These data are pseudonymous, not guaranteed anonymous. Firebase Analytics advertising-ID collection and advertising personalization are disabled for the game's developer analytics; this does not disable or change the separate AdMob advertising SDK. Analytics data is sent to Google over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections and processed under Google's policies and our analytics configuration. Previously sent data is not erased merely by turning collection off. See Google Analytics for Firebase data disclosure, Firebase collection controls, and Google's Privacy Policy.

Hosted platforms may separately handle platform activity, ads, cloud saves and leaderboard data through their own SDKs and policies. In particular, Playgama Bridge platform analytics is separate from this optional developer Google Analytics integration. The itch.io browser edition has no advertising or in-app purchases. An optional platform rating is handled by that platform; the game does not require positive feedback or offer rewards for ratings. Contact us with questions about developer analytics or data requests.

iOS edition

The iOS edition includes optional native Firebase Analytics using the consent controls described above. It collects an app-instance identifier, app/device information, approximate region derived from the network connection, and gameplay interaction/progression events for analytics. These data can be associated with an app installation and are not described as anonymous. The iOS edition has no advertisements, in-app purchases, external account login or online leaderboard. It does not request precise location, access to contacts, microphone or camera. Advertising identifier (IDFA), vendor identifier (IDFV), advertising storage and advertising personalization are disabled for this analytics integration. You can play offline and decline analytics without losing access to gameplay. Use Settings to withdraw consent; this disables analytics collection and resets locally stored analytics data. Previously transmitted analytics data is not automatically removed by uninstalling the game or withdrawing consent. Contact support for questions or data requests. See Firebase iOS collection controls.

Local Wi-Fi multiplayer

When you choose local multiplayer, the app uses your local network to host or join a room for up to six players. The host shares a local IP address, port and temporary room code through a link or QR code. Nearby guests can download the game assets directly from the host and exchange selected cars, room status, gameplay inputs and match messages. These connections use unencrypted HTTP/WebSocket on your local network; use a network you trust. The room is available while the host keeps the game open and is closed when the iOS app enters the background. No developer account, Internet matchmaking service, contact list, camera access or location permission is required. The app does not send room addresses, room codes or multiplayer packets to the developer or to Firebase. Local networking is independent of your optional analytics choice. You may decline local network permission and continue playing single-player; change that permission in device Settings.

Advertising and consent

The Android app uses Google AdMob for rewarded advertisements and advertisements between gameplay sessions. Google Mobile Ads may process your IP address (including approximate location), advertising/app-set or other device/account identifiers, ad and app interactions, and diagnostic/performance information to provide and measure advertising and prevent fraud. Data sent by the advertising SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. The game does not request precise device location.

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) manages advertising privacy choices. Where required, the app asks for consent before requesting ads. You can use the game's “Ad privacy options” setting to review or change available choices. You can reset or delete the advertising ID through Android settings. Advertising availability depends on network connectivity, your choices, and Google's service. See Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy for details about Google's handling and retention of data.

Google Play Games and leaderboards

You may choose to sign in to Google Play Games to use the career leaderboard. The game uses your Play Games player identifier and display name and submits your integer career score to Google. Your name, score, and ranking may be visible to other players according to your Google profile settings. The game does not maintain a separate server account or separately store your Google identity. Ordinary gameplay and local progress are available without leaderboard sign-in.

Google also processes Play Games profile and SDK service data. Leaderboard requests use encrypted connections. Manage visibility or delete game data through Google Play Games settings; clearing the local game save does not remove a leaderboard score held by Google. See Play Games SDK data information and Google's Privacy Policy.

Support and data requests

If you email support, we use your email address and message to respond and resolve the reported issue. Please do not send passwords, payment information, or unnecessary personal documents. You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the same address. Information may need to be retained to resolve a dispute or meet applicable obligations. For Google-managed advertising or Play Games data, use Google's privacy and account controls.

Audience and changes

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through support, contact us so we can review and delete it. This policy will be updated if the game's data practices change; the date above identifies the current version.
